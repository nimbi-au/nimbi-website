AML/CTF readiness check.

Work through the questions below and answer honestly. The Source column shows where each obligation sits in the AML/CTF Act and Rules, so you can check any answer against the legislation itself.

AreaReadiness questionSourceYour answer
EnrolmentHave you enrolled on AUSTRAC's Reporting Entity Roll? (The deadline for enrolment was 29 July 2026)AML/CTF Act s 51E
Risk AssessmentHave you conducted a risk assessment covering your services, customers, channels, jurisdictions and employee risk?s 26C
AML/CTF PoliciesDo you have written AML/CTF policies appropriate to your nature, size and complexity?s 26F
GovernanceHas a senior manager approved your program, with ongoing oversight by your governing body?ss 26H, 26P
Compliance OfficerHave you appointed a fit and proper AML/CTF compliance officer at management level?s 26J
Customer Due DiligenceDo you identify and verify each customer before providing a designated service?Pt 2
Ongoing MonitoringDo you monitor customers and transactions on an ongoing basis for suspicious activity?Pt 2
ReportingCan you lodge suspicious matter reports within 3 business days and threshold transaction reports for cash of $10,000 or more within 10 business days?ss 41, 43
Records & TrainingDo you keep records for 7 years, with personnel due diligence and AML/CTF training in place?Pt 10; s 26F(4)
Independent EvaluationIs an independent evaluation of your program scheduled at least every 3 years?s 26F(4)(f); R 5-10
