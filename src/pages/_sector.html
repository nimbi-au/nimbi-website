Your obligations - the same ten apply to every reporting entity

  1. Enrol on AUSTRAC's Reporting Entity Roll. The 29 July 2026 deadline has passed - unenrolled firms should act immediately. s 51E
  2. Undertake and document an ML/TF risk assessment covering your services, customers, delivery channels and countries. s 26C
  3. Maintain written AML/CTF policies appropriate to your nature, size and complexity. s 26F
  4. Have a senior manager approve the program, with ongoing oversight by your governing body. ss 26H, 26P
  5. Appoint a fit and proper AML/CTF compliance officer at management level. s 26J
  6. Identify and verify every customer before providing a designated service. Pt 2
  7. Monitor customers and transactions on an ongoing basis for unusual or suspicious activity. Pt 2
  8. Report suspicious matters within 3 business days (24 hours for terrorism financing) and cash transactions of $10,000 or more within 10 business days. ss 41, 43
  9. Make and keep records for 7 years, with personnel due diligence and AML/CTF training in place. Pt 10; s 26F(4)
  10. Have your program independently evaluated at least every 3 years. s 26F(4)(f); Rules s 5-10
