Who we help

The AML/CTF Act applies by service, not by profession. Each guide sets out when the rules may apply to your kind of practice, what the obligations look like in practice, and how Nimbi Foundations and Lens support you.

Accountants

Tax returns, financial statements, BAS and bookkeeping are not designated services on their own. Transactions, client money and structures are where the rules may apply.

Lawyers and conveyancers

Advice, litigation and dispute resolution on their own are generally outside the Act. Acting in a transaction or creating a structure is inside it.

Real estate

Agents, buyers' agents and developers selling their own developments provide designated services. Rent-only property management does not.
