How Nimbi helps

Foundations prepares the framework. Your team uses Lens to carry out customer due diligence. Your firm keeps the decisions and the accountability.

Simplified CDD is available only where the customer's ML/TF risk is low, enhanced CDD is not required and your AML/CTF policies set out the simplified measures; it is not an exemption from initial or ongoing CDD. Enhanced CDD is required when the Act triggers it, including high ML/TF risk, a foreign politically exposed person, a suspicious matter reporting obligation where you continue the service, and a FATF call-for-action jurisdiction, and it can apply in initial and ongoing CDD. Lens gathers and documents source-of-funds and wealth information; your team assesses it and decides whether it is established.

Nimbi Foundations: AML/CTF risk assessments, programs and implementation

Three layers. Know: the risk assessment, with a risk appetite, the reasoning written down and your existing client book mapped and risk-rated. Set: the AML/CTF program, policies, the compliance officer's role, approvals, training and the review cycle. Do: process guides, quick reference guides, CDD checklists, enhanced due diligence templates, decision records and registers. Delivery includes scoping and enrolment guidance, training, implementation and a 12-month review and support calendar. The portfolio mapping is advisory analysis of your existing clients; it is not a completed CDD review of each of them.

Nimbi Lens: self-service CDD, KYC and KYB tooling

Your team uses Lens to complete each review: identity and liveness checks, company and ownership information, PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening, questionnaires for the nature and purpose of the relationship and for source of funds and wealth, re-screening, and evidence you can export. Lens does not decide whether a customer is accepted, monitor transactions automatically, act as your compliance officer or lodge reports; those stay with your firm. Its evidence can support risk reviews, governance reporting and the independent evaluation.
