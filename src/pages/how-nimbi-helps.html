02NIMBI LENS

Know your customers’ risk profile.

Your team uses Lens to gather evidence, assess customer risk and complete customer due diligence (CDD), backed by your Foundations program and procedures.

The result: evidence to support a documented customer decision.

THE TOOLS BEHIND EACH CUSTOMER REVIEW

Verify

KYC, KYB and ownership information.

Screen

Initial and continuous PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening.

Understand

Questionnaires for funds, wealth and the customer relationship.

Assess

Customer risk ratings and evidence your team can review.

Lens does not decide whether a customer is accepted, monitor transactions automatically, act as your compliance officer or lodge reports; those stay with your firm.

03FOUNDATIONS + LENS

Keep the framework effective as things change.

Customer relationships and risks change. Lens supports continuous customer monitoring through screening and risk-review tools. Foundations gives your team the triggers and procedures for reviewing what those changes mean.

  • Review screening results and changes in customer circumstances.
  • Reassess risk and apply further checks when required.
  • Use documented evidence for oversight, reporting and evaluation.

Before you get started

Does the Act apply to my practice?

It applies by service, not by profession. If your practice provides a designated service in the course of business, such as a property transaction or setting up a company or trust, the Act applies. The readiness check and each sector guide list them plainly.

What is included in the $159 subscription?

Nimbi Foundations where your firm needs it, curated from your business inputs; the working kit: guides, checklists, templates, decision records and registers; Nimbi Lens; training, onboarding, implementation and support, for up to four users. Checks cost $19 per KYC and $45 per KYB.

What does Nimbi Comprehensive add?

A dedicated practitioner, a risk assessment, program and governance tailored end to end, your existing client book mapped and risk-rated, and a 12-month support calendar. It includes 12 months of the subscription with 60 KYC and 20 KYB checks. Fee quoted after a scoping call.

What stays with our firm?

The decisions. Your senior manager approves the program, your compliance officer runs it, your team decides whether to take on a client, and your firm lodges any reports and keeps the records. Nimbi builds the framework and tools; accountability under the Act stays with you.

How do we get started?

Book a scoping call. We confirm your services and subscription terms; you share your business details and any existing AML documents; Nimbi curates Foundations and guides your team into Lens. If enrolment is overdue, apply to AUSTRAC now; we help you prioritise the gaps.
