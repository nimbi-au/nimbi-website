A few things to know

Before you choose.

A few useful answers, when you need them.

Does Nimbi make our customer decisions?

No. Nimbi provides the framework, platform and agreed support. Lens does not automatically monitor transactions or lodge reports. Your firm assesses the evidence, decides whether to accept a customer or apply further measures, lodges its reports and retains accountability.

When would we use Nimbi Outsource?

When a legal entity has layered ownership or intermediate parents and your team would like help with the checks. Nimbi carries out the agreed checks and prepares a supporting file under an agency arrangement. We agree the scope, inclusions and fee in your proposal. Your firm reviews the evidence and retains its CDD decisions and accountability.

How much practitioner support do we need?

Foundations + Lens provides a framework curated from your business inputs and tools your team operates. Comprehensive adds a dedicated practitioner for custom delivery and existing client risk mapping. Nimbi Outsource is optional support for complex-entity checks and file preparation, quoted to your agreed scope.
