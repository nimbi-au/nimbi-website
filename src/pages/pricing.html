A few things to know

Frequently asked questions

Answers about plans, checks and support.

Which plan suits us if we already have an AML/CTF program?

Lite gives your team access to Lens with no subscription fee; you pay for the checks you use. If you also need a practitioner-curated framework, training and implementation support, consider Pro. Comprehensive adds custom delivery and existing client portfolio risk mapping.

What onboarding is included?

Lite includes platform onboarding to help you access and use Lens. Pro also includes guides and implementation onboarding. Comprehensive adds personalised practitioner support to implement your framework. AML/CTF training is a separately charged optional service on Lite and is included in Pro and Comprehensive.

What does standard KYB cover?

Standard KYB provides legal-entity and ownership information to support your customer assessment. Your team reviews the information and decides whether further checks are needed.

Does Nimbi make our customer decisions?

No. Nimbi provides the framework, platform and agreed support. Lens does not automatically monitor transactions or lodge reports. Your firm assesses the evidence, decides whether to accept a customer or apply further measures, lodges its reports and retains accountability.

When would we use Nimbi Outsource?

When a legal entity has layered ownership or intermediate parent entities and your team would like help with the checks. We carry out the agreed checks and prepare a supporting file under an agency agreement. Scope, inclusions and fees are agreed in your proposal. Your firm reviews the evidence and retains its CDD decisions, reporting obligations and accountability.

How much practitioner support do we need?

Pro includes a practitioner-curated framework, Lens, training, onboarding and a dedicated account manager. Comprehensive adds a custom program, personalised onboarding and training, existing client portfolio risk mapping and included checks. Nimbi Outsource provides separately quoted checks and supporting file preparation for complex ownership structures.
