Pricing details and what happens after 12 months

All prices are in AUD, excluding GST. The Foundations + Lens subscription is a 12-month term, billed monthly ($1,908 for the subscription over 12 months). KYC and KYB checks are charged separately at $19 and $45 per check.

The $3,948 Comprehensive inclusion value comprises 12 months of subscription ($1,908), 60 KYC checks ($1,140) and 20 KYB checks ($900). It is the value of the included subscription and checks, not the Comprehensive fee. Checks beyond the allowance are charged at standard per-check prices.

After the included 12 months, Comprehensive clients continue on the subscription at $159 a month plus checks. Renewal and record access after a subscription ends are governed by your engagement terms.
