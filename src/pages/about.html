Our story

Nimbi was founded by senior financial crime practitioners who have spent their careers designing and running AML/CTF programs inside Australia's largest reporting entities. When the Tranche 2 reforms brought accountants, real estate professionals, lawyers and conveyancers into the regime, we saw the same story repeating everywhere: capable firms, real obligations, and nobody to carry them.

So we built the firm we'd want to call. Practical, plain speaking, anchored to the legislation, and honest about what a small business actually needs - effective compliance solutions.
