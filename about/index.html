About us

We started Nimbi to help small and medium-sized businesses make sense of AML/CTF compliance and put it into practice.

Our team brings together experience in financial crime, risk management, project delivery and technology. Our background spans banking and financial services, with expertise in AML/CTF and sanctions, leading teams, and designing and developing technology solutions.

We know compliance is only one part of running a business. Our job is to explain what's needed, help you put practical processes in place and support you as your business grows. We want you and your team to feel comfortable asking questions, understand your obligations and feel confident managing compliance day to day.

What we stand on

Grounded in the legislation

Every recommendation traces back to the AML/CTF Act, the AML/CTF Rules 2025 or AUSTRAC guidance, with the source cited alongside.

Complex made clear

We're transparent about what your obligations mean, and what they don't. Our job is translating requirements into processes your team can run.

Right-sized for your business

The Act requires a program appropriate to your nature, size and complexity. We take the same approach to our advice and our fees.
