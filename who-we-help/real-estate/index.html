Start with your services

Understand where the obligations apply.

Your designated services determine whether obligations under the AML/CTF Act and Rules apply. Check the work you perform, its connection to Australia and any relevant exclusions or exemptions.

Sales agents

Brokering a property sale or transfer. Both buyer and seller are customers, even when you represent one.

Buyers' agents

Finding or negotiating property for a client. Obligations can begin before a property is selected.

Developers selling direct

Selling real estate as a business without an independent agent brokering the sale, including through in-house staff.

What sits outside these services, and when do checks start?

Rent-only management and construction alone are outside these real-estate services. A direct-selling developer's position depends on who brokers each sale. Agent relationships can begin when the agency agreement is signed; the counterparty becomes a customer when the transaction is reasonably expected to proceed. Initial CDD timing and any permitted delay must be checked for the circumstances.

Examples

Recognise the risk in everyday work.

Illustrative warning signs to investigate, not evidence of wrongdoing. Your team checks the facts and follows its procedures.

  • Unclear ownership

    A legal entity buyer cannot explain who ultimately owns or controls it.

    Obtain the ownership evidence and assess the gaps.

  • A late change

    The buyer or funding arrangement changes without a clear explanation.

    Review the new information and any checks it affects.

  • An unexplained purchase budget

    A buyer's agent receives a substantial purchase budget that conflicts with the client's stated finances, and the funding explanation changes.

    Clarify the funding source and resolve inconsistencies before making a customer decision.

  • Direct sale, unexplained deposit

    A developer selling directly receives an off-the-plan deposit from an unrelated entity, and the buyer cannot explain its involvement.

    Clarify the payer's connection, funding source and implications for the customer's risk.
