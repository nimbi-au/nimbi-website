Start with your services

Understand where the obligations apply.

Your designated services determine whether obligations under the AML/CTF Act and Rules apply. Check the work you perform, its connection to Australia and any relevant exclusions or exemptions.

Structures and appointments

Creating or restructuring legal entities and arrangements; officeholder, registered-address and shelf-company services.

Property, businesses and finance

Helping a client progress a property transaction, a transfer of controlling interests or entity financing.

Client money and property

Handling client assets as part of assisting a transaction. The purpose and applicable exceptions matter.

Which work needs a closer scope check?

Tax returns, BAS, financial statements and bookkeeping are not designated services by themselves. Work that directly advances a relevant transaction or structure may be. Client-money exceptions are conditional: incidental handling for non-designated work depends, among other things, on the entity providing no other designated services beyond that client-property service. Examples

Recognise the risk in everyday work.

Illustrative warning signs to investigate, not evidence of wrongdoing. Your team checks the facts and follows its procedures.

  • Layers without a clear reason

    An entity structure has several owners but no explained commercial purpose.

    Clarify the purpose, ownership and control.

  • An unexpected payer

    Funds arrive from someone unrelated to the engagement.

    Ask who is paying, why and what evidence supports the explanation.

  • An appointment without clear control

    A proposed director knows little about the business and takes instructions from someone whose ownership or control has not been explained.

    Clarify the appointment, who controls the entity and the supporting evidence.

  • Conflicting explanations during SMSF setup

    During an SMSF setup, a client gives conflicting explanations for the source of a substantial proposed contribution or asset transfer.

    Resolve the inconsistencies and document your assessment under the firm's procedures.
