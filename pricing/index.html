Do it yourself, buy software, or work with us

Relying on AUSTRAC's starter kit alone assumes you already know the risk typologies that matter, how they translate into risk themes, and how to map them to controls that actually reduce the inherent risk. That is not easy for a small or medium-sized reporting entity. Choosing and running the right software suits a firm that already knows what it wants, has the policies and procedures behind it, and has someone to run it. Nimbi is for the firm that wants a pragmatic risk assessment and client-book mapping, translated into effective controls and efficient processes.
