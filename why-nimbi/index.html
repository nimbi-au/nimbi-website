Why Nimbi

Compare your options.

Firms weighing up Nimbi usually consider two alternatives: doing it themselves with AUSTRAC's starter kit, or buying self-service AML software. Here's how each stacks up.

The starter kit is free. The work is not.

The AUSTRAC starter kit is a genuine starting point. The difference is who does the judgement, tailoring and implementation. Hover over any row, or tap it on a phone, for the detail behind the comparison.

Software runs checks. Nimbi builds judgement.

Self-service AML software helps a business generate and operate its AML documentation through a guided platform. Nimbi works with the business to understand its risks, design its compliance framework and embed it into day-to-day operations. Both are legitimate approaches - the difference is who carries the judgement. Hover over any row, or tap it on a phone, for the detail behind the comparison.

DimensionSelf-service AML softwareNimbi Foundations
Risk assessmentQuestionnaire and guided platform workflowConsultant-led discovery and analysis
A questionnaire can only ask the questions its designers anticipated. Our practitioners ask the follow-ups - how client money actually moves, who touches it, what unusual would look like in your firm - and build the assessment from what we find, not just what a form collects.
MethodologyA pre-built framework applied to your sectorTailored assessment of the firm's actual services, customers, channels, jurisdictions and operating model
No two firms in the same sector carry the same risk - a suburban conveyancer and one acting for offshore investors have little in common. A sector framework rates the average firm. We assess your actual services, clients, channels and jurisdictions, so the ratings mean something.
AML/CTF programSystem-generated from configured responsesPolicies, procedures, controls and governance designed around the business
A generated program describes a standard process, not necessarily yours. Any gap between what the documents say and what your firm actually does is exactly what an independent evaluation will find. We design the program around how you genuinely operate, so document and reality match.
Who challenges your assumptions?No one - the output reflects whatever information you enterA specialist tests assumptions, identifies gaps and challenges risk ratings before anything is finalised
Software treats your answers as facts: rate a risk too low going in and the platform confidently builds your entire program on the error. A practitioner's job is to catch it first - testing your assumptions is what makes the final program defensible.
ImplementationYou configure and implement it yourself - templated output built from your own responses, so critical substance can be missedApproximately 35 hours committed by Nimbi's AML/CTF specialists across assessment, program, training and implementation. Your time is limited to the fact find, workshops and review
The work exists either way - the question is who carries it. Self-service means partners spending evenings configuring a platform. With Foundations, our specialists carry around 35 hours of assessment, drafting, training and implementation, and your time is limited to fact find, workshops and review.
TrainingStandardised platform modules and assessmentsTailored training using the firm's roles, procedures, risks and practical scenarios
Generic modules teach definitions. Effective training teaches your people to recognise the moment a matter on their own desk becomes a red flag - so we train with your services, your client types and your escalation paths. It also makes your training records credible evidence, not a tick-box.
Operational embeddingPlatform workflows and supportTemplates, quick-reference guides, reporting bundle, governance responsibilities and implementation support
A program only works if the person onboarding a client on a Friday afternoon knows exactly what to do without opening a policy. We build that layer - checklists, quick-reference guides, reporting templates and a governance rhythm - so the program runs in daily practice, not just on paper.
Your existing client bookRisk ratings build client by client as you enter them - the back book you already hold stays unmapped unless you work through it yourselfYour full client book segmented, screened and risk-rated into tiers, giving each client a review cadence and your firm a defensible ongoing monitoring baseline from day one
Ongoing CDD applies to the clients you already hold, not just the next one through the door. Working through a back book client by client is the job nobody gets to. We segment, screen and risk-rate the whole book up front, so your highest-risk existing relationships surface now - not in three years.
Ongoing complianceYour program lives inside the platform - if the subscription ends, so does the system operating itYour program, policies and records are yours, whatever verification technology you use now or later
Generally, AML/CTF records must be kept for seven years - most software subscriptions don't last that long. If your program lives inside a platform, ending the subscription ends the system that operates it. Everything Nimbi builds sits in your own files and survives any change of technology.

The starter kit may suit the simplest practice, and software may suit the most confident. Foundations is for firms that want the judgement, tailoring and implementation done with them.
